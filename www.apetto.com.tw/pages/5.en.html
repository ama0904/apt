Shipping times

Once your order is confirmed, it takes about 3–6 working days from payment to delivery (excluding holidays).

On weekdays (Mon–Fri), from the day after we receive your payment, we ship within three days once your details are confirmed.

Please note that we do not ship on weekends or public holidays.
